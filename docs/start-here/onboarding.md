# Onboarding

Work through this list with your assigned lab mentor.

## Before your first day

- Get a NetID and UW email address
- Get on the lab slack

## First day

- Get a wiscard
- Connect to wifi
- Connect to the printer
- Request building and lab card access
- Request lab keys
- Start required trainings

## First week

- Get added to the lab biosafety protocol and sign the training binder
- Add the lab calendar
- Request a CHTC Account on the Job Board
- Join the ResearchDrive
- Sign up for iLab and get BIF access
- Get a bus pass
- Add the VPN
