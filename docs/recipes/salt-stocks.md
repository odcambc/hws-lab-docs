!!! danger "Unvalidated recipes"
    Verify reagent identity, formula weight, hydrate state, purity, hazards,
    sterilization compatibility, and storage period before preparation.
    Concentrated salt dissolution may generate substantial heat.

## General preparation

1. Confirm the exact chemical form on the bottle.
2. Record the manufacturer, catalog number, lot, hydrate state, and formula
   weight.
3. Begin with less than the final volume of suitable water.
4. Add the solid gradually with stirring and control the temperature.
5. After dissolution and cooling, bring to final volume.
6. Sterilize only by an approved method.
7. Label with composition, concentration, preparation date, expiration,
   preparer, and storage condition.
