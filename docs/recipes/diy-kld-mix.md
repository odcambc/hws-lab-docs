# DIY KLD mix

## Purpose

KLD mix gets expensive from NEB. It's essentially just a kinase, a ligase, and DpnI, so we can make our own.

## Reagents

NEB sells all these.

* T4 Polynucleotide Kinase
* T4 DNA Ligase
* DpnI
