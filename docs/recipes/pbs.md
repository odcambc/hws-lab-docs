# 1× phosphate-buffered saline

!!! danger "Demonstration content only"
    Verify this formulation against the lab's validated recipe before use.

## Formula

| Component | 1 L |
|---|---:|
| Sodium chloride | 8.0 g |
| Potassium chloride | 0.2 g |
| Disodium phosphate | 1.44 g |
| Monopotassium phosphate | 0.24 g |

## Preparation

1. Dissolve components in approximately 800 mL purified water.
2. Adjust pH according to the validated lab specification.
3. Bring to 1 L with purified water.
4. Label with name, concentration, preparation date, expiration date, and
   preparer.
