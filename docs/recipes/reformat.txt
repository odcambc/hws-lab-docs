Recipes
General notes
Hydrates: Hydrated forms are easier to dissolve. Note that the molecular weight (and thus recipe) will vary based on hydrate.
Salts
Calcium chloride (CaCl₂) – 2.5 M
Choose form (per 1 L):
Anhydrous CaCl₂: 277.5 g/L
CaCl₂·2H₂O: 367.6 g/L
Sterilize: 0.22 µm filter or autoclave
Storage: RT


Notes: Record hydrate form; solutions can warm during dissolution.
Warning: Dissolving is exothermic
Ammonium chloride (NH₄Cl) – 4 M
For 1 L: 214.0 g NH₄Cl → bring to 1.0 L
Sterilize: 0.22 µm filter or autoclave
Storage: RT

Sterilize: Autoclave or 0.22 µm filter
Storage: RT


Notes: Hygroscopic–cap promptly after weighing.
Warning: Dissolving is exothermic

Other
DTT (dithiothreitol) – 1 M in sodium acetate buffer
Example prep (20 mL): 3.09 g DTT → dissolve and bring to 20.0 mL in 10–20 mM sodium acetate, pH 5.2
Sterilize: 0.22 µm filter
Aliquot: Yes (e.g., 0.5–1 mL)
Storage: −20 °C


Notes: Minimize freeze–thaw; protect from oxidation (tight caps, small headspace).


IPTG – 1 M
Stock concentration: 1 M
Prep: 2.4 g IPTG → bring to 10.0 mL with water
Sterilize: 0.22 µm filter
Aliquot: Yes
Storage: −20 °C
Glycerol – 10% to 50% (v/v)
Concentration: As needed (10–50% v/v)
Prep: Measure glycerol by volume; bring to final volume with water
Easier: use density of glycerol (1.26 g/mL) and measure by weight
Ex: 1 L 50% glycerol solution: 500 mL (g) H2O + 630 g glycerol
Sterilize: Autoclave
Storage: RT
TCEP - 0.5 M
Reagent: TCEP·HCl (check bottle; MW varies slightly by salt form–use label MW)
Workflow: dissolve in water → adjust pH ~7 (if needed) → bring to volume
Sterilize: 0.22 µm filter
Aliquot: yes (e.g., 0.5–1 mL)
Store: −20 °C
Notes: more stable than DTT in many buffers; avoid repeated freeze–thaw.
1.0 M L-arginine, pH 8.5 (common)
Reagent: L-arginine free base (MW 174.20)
For 1 L: 174.2 g
Dissolve in ~800 mL water (stir well)
Adjust to pH 8.5 with NaOH
Bring to 1.0 L


Sterilize: Autoclave
Store: RT


Important: If using arginine-HCl, the grams/L differs–don’t substitute.
