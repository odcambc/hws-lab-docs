# Wayment-Steele Lab protocols

Internal documentation for routine laboratory work. Use the navigation menu or
search to find a protocol, recipe, instrument guide, or general lab procedure.

!!! warning "Before using a protocol"
    Confirm that the page is marked **Approved**, check its review date, and
    read the complete procedure and safety notes before starting work.

## Contents

- [Protocols](protocols/index.md) — experimental methods and quality checks
- [Recipes](recipes/index.md) — buffers, media, and stock solutions
- [Instruments](instruments/index.md) — operating and maintenance instructions
- [General lab information](general/index.md) — ordering, safety, and routine
  lab operations
- [Onboarding](training/onboarding.md) — required setup and training

## Document status

**Approved** pages have completed the lab's review process. **Draft** and
**Needs review** pages are working documents and must not be treated as
validated procedures.
