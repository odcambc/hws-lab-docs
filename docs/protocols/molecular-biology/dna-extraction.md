## Purpose

Illustrate how a working protocol can combine preparation, stepwise
instructions, safety guidance, stopping points, and quality control.

## Before starting

### Materials

| Item | Working amount | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Cell sample | 1 pellet | Keep on ice |
| Lysis buffer | 500 µL | Prepare fresh |
| Proteinase K | 20 µL | Keep chilled |
| Elution buffer | 100 µL | Pre-warm to 56 °C |

### Equipment

- Benchtop microcentrifuge
- Heat block set to 56 °C
- Calibrated pipettes

!!! warning "Safety"
    Wear the required PPE and consult the applicable safety documentation for
    every reagent. Perform hazardous manipulations only in the designated
    containment area.

## Procedure

### 1. Lyse the sample

1. Resuspend the pellet in **500 µL lysis buffer**.
2. Add **20 µL Proteinase K** and mix by gentle inversion.
3. Incubate at **56 °C for 30 minutes**.

!!! tip "Good technique"
    Avoid vigorous vortexing after lysis if high-molecular-weight DNA is
    important downstream.

### 2. Clarify and wash

1. Centrifuge using the validated settings for the sample type.
2. Transfer the clarified material without disturbing debris.
3. Complete the validated wash sequence.

### 3. Elute

1. Add **100 µL pre-warmed elution buffer**.
2. Incubate for 5 minutes.
3. Collect the eluate in a clearly labeled low-bind tube.

!!! success "Stopping point"
    Store the eluate under the conditions validated for the downstream
    application.

## Quality control

Record the yield, purity measurements, sample identifier, operator, and any
deviation from the protocol.

| Check | Example acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Appearance | Clear, without visible debris |
| A260/A280 | 1.8–2.0 |
| Traceability | Sample and run records complete |

## Troubleshooting

??? question "Yield is lower than expected"
    Confirm starting material, resuspension, reagent age, incubation settings,
    and whether any material was lost during transfers.

??? question "The sample contains visible debris"
    Do not improvise an extra processing step. Record the observation and
    consult the protocol owner.
