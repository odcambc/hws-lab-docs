# Travel and conferences

As soon as you’re at a point where you have new research to put on a poster, it’s the goal/expectation that lab members go to conferences. Hannah will pay for members to go to 1 conference/year, with the exception of certain local conferences / Rosetta events that are discounted. This isn’t hard and fast – if there’s something you really want to go to and want to make a case that goes beyond that, talk with Hannah.

## Quick guide

| Where are you going?                             | What to do                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| A conference (or anything you are presenting at) | Book travel through concur and reimburse conference fees |
| A work-related thing you are not presenting at   | Book travel through concur and reimburse travel fees     |
| Anything unclear                                 | Ask on Slack or contact Biochemistry travel team         |

See the
[Biochemistry travel & expense reimbursement intranet page](https://uwprod.sharepoint.com/sites/biochem/travel) and the [Biochemistry travel page](https://uwprod.sharepoint.com/sites/biochem/travel/SitePages/Travel.aspx)
for information from the department.

## Detailed guide

### What can be expensed

Conference registration, travel, lodging, and per diem for food.

### How to pay for a conference

Submit a [pre-trip form](https://uwmadison.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eXOzBGgWBHivLQa) to Biochem

 You will need the conference program, proof of an abstract or presentation, the funding details, and a description of the point of the travel

#### Registration fees

To prepay airfare or registration fees, book an appointment with the travel team. Request an appointment by inviting travel@biochem.wisc.edu to a meeting in the Outlook calendar.

If membership in some society gives a discount you can pay for it at the same time.

#### Flights

To prepay airfare or registration fees, book an appointment with the travel team. Request an appointment by inviting travel@biochem.wisc.edu to a meeting in the Outlook calendar.

You can also pay yourself and get it reimbursed, but you will have to wait then.

Flights _must_ be purchased on [Concur](https://us2.concursolutions.com/)!

### Lodging

Lodging can only be paid in advance if it's part of the conference fee. You need to pay for it and get it reimbursed.

Lodging _must_ be purchased through Concur, the conference website, or a hotel website/Airbnb directly! Third-party sites can't be reimbursed.

#### Cars

If you're driving yourself you can get milage reimbursed. It can't be done in advance.

If you rent a car you need to book it yourself through Concur. It can't be paid for in advance and it won't be reimbursable if you don't book it through Concur.

#### Transit/other travel

You can get cabs or trains covered. They aren't reimbursable in advance.

Parking is reimbursable but can't be paid for in advance.

#### Per diem

Locations have a certain per diem for food that you can get reimbursed. You don't need receipts, but you also only get a fixed amount per day.

## Getting reimbursed

Use the [reimbursement request form](https://uwprod.sharepoint.com/sites/biochem/travel/SitePages/Expense-Reimbursement.aspx) to start a request. Biochem will prepare it for you. You will need records of payments.

## What fund to use

If you are travelling for a specific program, use that one. Otherwise use the standard/startup ones.

Check the [Funding info sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1az6nKcqeddkzBbQE5lOFLplK8zDWnqcYp45awjie94s/edit?usp=sharing) for specific codes.

## Travel grants

If you're an undergrad, grad student or postdoc, you should apply for a travel award. [Biochemistry will grant up to one per year](https://uwprod.sharepoint.com/sites/biochem/travel/SitePages/Biochemistry-Travel-Awards.aspx) if you're presenting or have your own poster. You need to submit the request at least one week in advance.

Grad students are also eligible for [Student Research Grants Competition](https://grad.wisc.edu/funding/grants-competition/). You can apply for one even if you're not going to a conference, as long as it's research for your thesis. These are on a quarterly basis, so you need to apply in advance for travel that's within a particular cycle - check the eligibility!
