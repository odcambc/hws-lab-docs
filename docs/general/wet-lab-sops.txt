Autoclaving
Generally: 121°C at 15 psi for some period of time (30 minutes often). This may need to be increased if the chamber is very full.

Liquids use a slow exhaust. Solids and dry goods (pipette tips, etc) use fast exhaust.

Autoclave tape only shows that the chamber reached a temperature, now how long it was there.

Don’t autoclave:
Polystyrene
Polyethylene
Polyurethane
