## Antibiotics for plasmid selection

| Antibiotic | Legacy working concentration |
|---|---:|
| Ampicillin | 100 µg/mL |
| Carbenicillin | 100 µg/mL |
| Chloramphenicol | 33 µg/mL |
| Kanamycin | 30 µg/mL |
| Streptomycin | 25 µg/mL |
| Tetracycline | 15 µg/mL |

The legacy notes recommend carbenicillin in place of ampicillin where
appropriate. Confirm the plasmid marker, host background, application, and
approved lab standard before preparing selective medium.

## Centrifuge rotor note

!!! danger "JLA-10.500 rotor"
    Install a bucket in **all six rotor positions**, even when fewer than six
    sample bottles are being centrifuged. Unused buckets may be empty, but the
    rotor must not be operated with a bucket position vacant. Balance the
    loaded assembly according to the manufacturer and local instrument SOP.
