# Onboarding

Work through this list with your assigned lab mentor. Some access requests depend
on UW appointment status, so start the university steps first and follow up if a
form cannot yet verify your record.

## University essentials

- Get a NetID and UW email address.
- Get a Wiscard. See the [Wiscard Office instructions](https://wiscard.wisc.edu/id-card/how-to-get-your-wiscard/). Faculty and staff records must be entered by the department before a card can be issued.
- Enroll in benefits if eligible. Confirm the deadline in Workday soon after joining.
- Get a campus bus pass through [UW Transportation Services](https://transportation.wisc.edu/commuter-solutions/bus/#buspasses).

## Building and lab access

- Request Wiscard access for the Biochemical Sciences Building with the [building access form](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=IYOmLNoOCEmIskJKjLSw-eb7JFjkUs9AmKUsejVsZCpUM0QxVFlDODlOUTlBUjhPTVQ0QVpGQlFYSC4u).
- Request keys for BSB 2206 and BSB 2204 with the [key request form](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=IYOmLNoOCEmIskJKjLSw-eb7JFjkUs9AmKUsejVsZCpUMFA3MkJHSVVPVExBTzlOS1lNRzJPVzRaUS4u&route=shorturl).
- Connect to wifi. Use [eduroam](https://it.wisc.edu/services/wireless-eduroam/) when possible; [UWNet](https://it.wisc.edu/services/wireless-uwnet/) is also available.
- Connect to the lab printer at `128.104.117.113`. You may need the Biochemistry VPN or a wired connection.

## Required training and safety

- Complete required UW safety trainings. Workday may assign required items; additional trainings are listed by [UW Environment, Health & Safety](https://ehs.wisc.edu/training/).
- Complete Biosafety Required Training if you will work under the lab biosafety protocol.
- Ask the chemical safety lead to add you to the lab biosafety protocol.
- Sign the training binder after protocol-specific training is complete.
- Optional: request ChemManager access with the [ChemManager request form](https://forms.office.com/r/Q22uPJSBzj). Use Hannah as PI, Chris as optional contact, and Lab Member as the role.

## Lab systems and shared resources

- Join the lab Slack. Use an email address you will continue to access.
- Ask to be added to the lab website if you are not added during onboarding.
- Add the lab calendar.
- Request access to the lab resources folder: `WS_lab_resources`.
- Join the lab ResearchDrive.
- Request a CHTC account through the [Biochemistry Job Board](https://jobboard.biochem.wisc.edu/).
- Sign up for [iLab](https://uwmadison.ilab.agilent.com/landing/1802) and request BIF access through the [Biophysics Instrumentation Facility](https://bif.wisc.edu/) if your work requires it.
- Optional: set up ordering access in Workday if you will purchase lab supplies.

## Network and remote access

- Install the [Biochemistry VPN](https://kb.wisc.edu/biochem/it/vpn) for department network access.
- Install the [general UW VPN](https://it.wisc.edu/services/wiscvpn/) if needed for campus services.
- Add the library proxy [bookmarklet](https://kb.wisc.edu/library/4773) for journal access.

## First-week review

Ask your mentor where to find current guides, SOPs, and lab duties. Review this
protocol library, especially the Start here, General lab info, and relevant
protocol sections, and ask questions before beginning unfamiliar work.
